# Module kuicksilver-ktor-client

The Ktor client engine `HttpClient(Kuicksilver)`: HTTP/3, and HTTP/2 or HTTP/1.1 over TLS, with Alt-Svc and HTTPS
record discovery, racing and fallback. Guide: `docs/guide/ktor.md`.

# Package io.kuicksilver.ktor.client

`Kuicksilver` (the engine factory), `KuicksilverEngineConfig` and its HTTP/3, proxy, Alt-Svc and diagnostics options,
the event listener and the protocol diagnostics of a response.
