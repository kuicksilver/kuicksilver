class Http3Settings(source)

webTransportInitialMaxData
Link copied to clipboard
val webTransportInitialMaxData: Long

SETTINGS_WT_INITIAL_MAX_DATA (section 5.5.3): the stream body bytes the peer may send in each WebTransport session, raised with WT_MAX_DATA as they are consumed: taken by the application, all of a stream that ended cleanly once it was handed to its input, a reset or stopped stream's by its final size. Our sender serves a non-incremental session's streams in the order they were opened and shares an incremental session's credit between them (RFC 9218); otherwise, or with a peer of another implementation, read the streams concurrently, or a strictly sequential reader under a tight limit can stall the session (Builder.webTransportInitialMaxData).

webTransportMaxSessions
Link copied to clipboard
val webTransportMaxSessions: Int

WebTransport sessions a server accepts at once on one connection when flow control is on (draft-ietf-webtrans-http3-16 section 5.2); one more is reset with H3_REQUEST_REJECTED before the application sees it. Without flow control a connection carries one session whatever this says (section 5.1). Advertised to draft-14 clients as SETTINGS_WT_MAX_SESSIONS. Ignored by a client, whose limit is the server's.
