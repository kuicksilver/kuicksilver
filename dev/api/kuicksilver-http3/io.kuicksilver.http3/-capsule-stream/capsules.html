kuicksilver-http3/io.kuicksilver.http3/CapsuleStream/capsules

capsules

abstract val capsules: Flow<Capsule>(source)

Capsules of CapsuleConfig.knownTypes from the peer, in order. One collector at a time. The flow ends normally at the peer's FIN on a capsule boundary; it fails with Http3Exception.MessageError when the FIN cuts a capsule (RFC 9297 section 3.3), a known capsule exceeds CapsuleConfig.maxCapsuleLength, or anything follows a capsule of CapsuleConfig.terminalTypes (which is delivered first), after the stream was reset and stopped with H3_MESSAGE_ERROR; and with the stream's or connection's error when the peer reset the stream or the connection closed. While nobody collects, reading pauses after CapsuleConfig.receiveQueue capsules, DATAGRAM capsules behind them included. A collector cancelled while it waits or handles a capsule leaves the next one for the next collector; a wrapper cancelled after a capsule was delivered (withTimeout around first()) may still drop that one, so collect in one long-lived coroutine. Operators that collect ahead in another coroutine (buffer, flowOn, conflate, produceIn, shareIn, stateIn, and collectLatest by design) hold capsules they took and have not delivered yet; those are lost when the collection is cancelled, so collect this flow directly.
