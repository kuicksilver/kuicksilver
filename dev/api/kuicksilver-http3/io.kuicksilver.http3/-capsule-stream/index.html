CapsuleStream

interface CapsuleStream(source)

An Extended CONNECT stream speaking the Capsule Protocol (RFC 9297 section 3), from Http3Stream.useCapsuleProtocol. The stream's data is a sequence of capsules both ways; HTTP datagrams arrive and leave as QUIC DATAGRAM frames or as DATAGRAM capsules. Once this object exists, stream's input, output and datagrams belong to it: use capsules, send, datagrams and the datagram senders here instead.

send, sendDatagram when it falls back to a capsule, and close have no deadline of their own: they wait for the peer's flow-control credit, which a peer that stops reading may withhold for as long as it keeps the connection alive; only a reset of the stream or a closed connection fails them. Bound them with withTimeout and Http3Stream.reset the stream when it expires.

capsules
Link copied to clipboard
abstract val capsules: Flow<Capsule>

Capsules of CapsuleConfig.knownTypes from the peer, in order. One collector at a time. The flow ends normally at the peer's FIN on a capsule boundary; it fails with Http3Exception.MessageError when the FIN cuts a capsule (RFC 9297 section 3.3), a known capsule exceeds CapsuleConfig.maxCapsuleLength, or anything follows a capsule of CapsuleConfig.terminalTypes (which is delivered first), after the stream was reset and stopped with H3_MESSAGE_ERROR; and with the stream's or connection's error when the peer reset the stream or the connection closed. While nobody collects, reading pauses after CapsuleConfig.receiveQueue capsules, DATAGRAM capsules behind them included. A collector cancelled while it waits or handles a capsule leaves the next one for the next collector; a wrapper cancelled after a capsule was delivered (withTimeout around first()) may still drop that one, so collect in one long-lived coroutine. Operators that collect ahead in another coroutine (buffer, flowOn, conflate, produceIn, shareIn, stateIn, and collectLatest by design) hold capsules they took and have not delivered yet; those are lost when the collection is cancelled, so collect this flow directly.

close

abstract suspend fun close()

Writes the capsules still queued, then ends our side of the stream (FIN); capsules keeps reading the peer's side. Throws the write's error if the queue could not be written. Calling it again does nothing more.

send
Link copied to clipboard
abstract suspend fun send(capsule: Capsule)

Sends capsule, whole and never interleaved with another; suspends while CapsuleConfig.sendQueue capsules wait. Throws IllegalStateException after close, and the write's error once writing failed (a reset, STOP_SENDING, a closed connection). No deadline of its own: bound it with withTimeout and Http3Stream.reset the stream when it expires; a capsule already queued when the caller gives up may still be written.

abstract suspend fun sendDatagram(payload: ByteArray)

trySendDatagram that waits for room: in the transport's queue, or in the capsule queue when falling back. Throws as Http3Stream.sendDatagram does, and IllegalStateException after close. When falling back it may wait for the peer's flow-control credit: bound it with withTimeout and Http3Stream.reset the stream when it expires.

trySendDatagram
Link copied to clipboard
abstract fun trySendDatagram(payload: ByteArray): DatagramSendResult

Sends payload as a QUIC DATAGRAM when HTTP/3 datagrams are available, answering as Http3Stream.trySendDatagram. When they are unsupported and CapsuleConfig.datagramFallback is on, it goes as a DATAGRAM capsule instead (RFC 9297 section 3.5): Sent once queued, QueueFull while CapsuleConfig.sendQueue capsules wait. TooLarge is returned as is, never retried as a capsule. Closed after close or once the send side is closed.
