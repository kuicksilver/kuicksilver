Bbr

class Bbr(maxDatagramSize: Int = 1200, random: Random? = null) : CongestionController(source)

BBR congestion control as specified by draft-ietf-ccwg-bbr (BBRv3): a model-based controller that paces at its estimate of the bottleneck bandwidth and keeps about one bandwidth-delay product in flight, instead of reacting to every loss.

The model is a windowed maximum of delivery-rate samples (two ProbeBW cycles) and a windowed minimum RTT (10 s, refreshed by ProbeRTT every 5 s), bounded by loss: a probe that loses more than 2 % of what it had in flight caps the long-term inflight (inflight_longterm), and every round with loss outside a probe scales the short-term bounds (bw_shortterm, inflight_shortterm) by 0.7. The state machine is Startup, Drain, ProbeBW (Down, Cruise, Refill, Up) and ProbeRTT. ECN-CE counts as loss for the round (the draft leaves the ECN response open but requires CE to be treated as congestion), and a CE mark while probing ends the probe as excess loss would.

Experimental and opt-in: quicConfig { congestionController = { Bbr() } }. BBR tolerates random loss far better than Cubic and keeps the bottleneck queue short, but it shares a bottleneck with loss-based flows less predictably (it can take more or less than its share depending on the buffer size). A loss episode whose every lost packet is acknowledged later (reordering, a delay spike) is undone (section 5.5.11). Sizes are bytes, rates bytes per second, times nanoseconds; the arithmetic saturates instead of wrapping. Nothing here allocates after construction.

random desynchronises the bandwidth probes of flows sharing a bottleneck (the 2-3 s probe wait); without one the jitter is derived from the send and ACK times, which differ between flows.
