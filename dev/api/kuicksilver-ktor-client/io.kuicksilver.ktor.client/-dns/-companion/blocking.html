blocking

fun blocking(dispatcher: CoroutineDispatcher = blockingLookups, lookup: (host: String) -> List<IpAddress>): Dns(source)

A Dns over a blocking lookup (an OkHttp Dns, a call into a native resolver), run on dispatcher: by default a view of Dispatchers.IO limited to 8 threads, shared by every resolver made this way in the process, whatever engine uses it, so lookups that stall hold at most those and never the threads the engine's sockets run on. The other side of that bound: 8 lookups that hang hold all 8 threads, and every other lookup of the default pool, of any engine, waits behind them until one returns (their calls fail at their connect timeouts). Give a resolver that may hang a dispatcher of its own to keep it from starving the others. A caller that is cancelled or whose connect timeout ends stops waiting at once and takes its lookup off the queue if it has not started; a started blocking call cannot be interrupted, it goes on to its end on its thread and its answer is dropped.
