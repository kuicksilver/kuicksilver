TlsConnection

class TlsConnection : AutoCloseable(source)

TLS over any byte transport (docs/guide/tls.md, TLS over any channel): ciphertext moves through transportIn/transportOut, plaintext through input/output. Two pump coroutines and one mutex guard the single ksl_ssl. The transport is the caller's: sockets, a QUIC stream, anything that offers channels.

suspend fun shutdown()

Sends what the application wrote and close_notify, waits for the peer's, then releases the native session. The wait is bounded: after 3 s in all, also when the peer stopped reading and the transport is full, the connection is closed (close) with whatever was not sent.
