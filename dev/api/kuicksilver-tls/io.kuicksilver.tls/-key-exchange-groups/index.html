KeyExchangeGroups

class KeyExchangeGroups(groups: List<String>, keyShares: Set<String>? = null)(source)

The key-exchange groups of a TLS configuration (RFC 9846 sections 4.3.7 and 4.3.8), most preferred first, and the groups a client sends a key share for in its first ClientHello. Every invalid value throws TlsException.Config here; a group the OpenSSL in use does not provide throws it when the configuration is built (TlsConfig.Builder.build).

  • A client offers KeyExchangeGroups.groups in this order in supported_groups and key shares for KeyExchangeGroups.keyShares, in the same order. A server it shares no group with ends the handshake with handshake_failure (40; over QUIC, CRYPTO_ERROR 0x0128); one that takes a group the client sent no share for answers with a HelloRetryRequest (RFC 9846 section 4.2.1), which costs a round trip and rejects 0-RTT (RFC 9001 section 4.6.2).

  • A server picks by its own order: among the groups the client sent a key share for, the first of KeyExchangeGroups.groups; with none of them, a HelloRetryRequest for the first of KeyExchangeGroups.groups the client supports; with no group in common, handshake_failure. It ignores KeyExchangeGroups.keyShares. An sniSelector answer may bring its own (ServerTlsOverride.keyExchangeGroups).

  • TLS 1.2 (TlsVersions.Tls12And13, TCP) takes the elliptic curves of KeyExchangeGroups.groups (X25519, X448, SECP256R1, SECP384R1, SECP521R1) in the same order; the hybrids are TLS 1.3 only. There the list also names the curves of the peer's ECDSA certificates an endpoint accepts (RFC 8422 section 5.1): a client without SECP256R1 refuses a TLS 1.2 server's P-256 certificate with handshake_failure, and a server without it refuses a P-256 client certificate with illegal_parameter (47; OpenSSL holds client certificates to the server's own list). TLS 1.3 negotiates signatures apart.

A session ticket does not depend on the group: a resumption runs a fresh key exchange (psk_dhe_ke) under the groups in effect.

Parameters

groups

the group names, most preferred first: the constants of the companion, in any case; never empty, each at most once (RFC 9846 section 4.3.7: "The "named_group_list" MUST NOT contain any duplicate entries").

keyShares

the groups of KeyExchangeGroups.groups a client sends key shares for, 1 to 4 of them (each share is sent whole: an X25519MLKEM768 share alone is 1,216 bytes). Null: the first group, and the first later one of the other kind (hybrid or classical) when the first is of one, so a client offers a post-quantum and a classical share as BoringSSL does. Servers refuse a configuration that sets it.
